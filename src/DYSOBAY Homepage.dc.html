The midnight rebellion

Three in the morning.
Haifa was wide awake.

No fashion school had taught her the rules, so she never learned to follow them. While everyone else asked what sells, she asked what is missing. The answer was simple: everything. Fashion had forgotten how to be personal.

Every time she made something beautiful, the world wanted to multiply it. She said no. Each piece exists once — never repeated, never diluted. If everyone can have it, it is not worth having.
